Every post here is an `.mdx` file in `src/content/blog/`. Astro's content
collections give the folder a schema, so a post missing a `title` or a valid
`pubDate` fails the build instead of shipping broken.

## The frontmatter contract

```yaml
---
title: "Post Title"
description: "One line for the card and the meta tag."
pubDate: 2026-08-18
tags: ["tag-one", "tag-two"]
draft: false
---
```

`draft: true` keeps a post out of the listing and the RSS feed without
deleting it — handy for half-finished ideas.

## Why MDX over plain Markdown

MDX lets you drop in a real component when prose isn't enough — a chart, a
callout, an embed — while still writing everything else as normal Markdown.
Nothing here uses that yet, but the door's open.
